# Project brief

**The Listening Room · Katherine Lin**

This summary records the scope of an independently commissioned design project. Katherine Lin led the UX and interaction design.

## Role

Research, interaction design, prototyping, the voice search interaction specification and the evaluation plan. Recognition models, production catalogue integration and Bluetooth firmware were outside this scope.

## Objective

Design a music listening experience that helps older adults find and play personally meaningful music with greater confidence and independence. Prioritise the everyday moments when listeners return to a familiar song, choose a playlist or recover when a control or recognition result surprises them.

## Audience and design context

The design targets listeners aged 65 and over, with particular attention to low digital confidence, changing vision or dexterity, and different language and voice-search needs. Spotify is the reference interaction context. The design pairs a simplified phone experience with a tactile playback remote, MusicTag.

## Design scope

- Identify barriers to accessing saved songs, playing music and sharing with family.
- Design stable navigation, explicit playback controls and accessible display preferences.
- Define a voice search path that supports visible listening, match review, retry and a typed fallback.
- Explore how a physical remote can handle transport and volume while the phone remains responsible for choosing music and audio output.
- Demonstrate setup, failure and recovery through an interactive prototype.

## Deliverables and evaluation

The resulting deliverables are a high-fidelity interactive prototype, 24 iOS design states, an early phone and MusicTag concept, research synthesis, design rationale, a voice search specification and an evaluation plan. The project delivers a browser-based prototype with simulated service and hardware behaviour. The next evaluation tests whether intended listeners can complete and recover from the core tasks independently.

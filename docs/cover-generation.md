## Final prompt

> Use case: product-mockup. Create a NEW photographic UX portfolio panoramic hero image, exactly 2172 x 724 pixels, 3:1 aspect ratio, for the MusicTag music listening project for older adults, formerly The Listening Room. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT ANYWHERE: no title, no captions, no slogans, no wordmarks, no letters, no numbers, no readable text even on phone screens, no watermarks. The reference images supply visual product identity only. Make this an immersive edge-to-edge fully occupied composition, not a title slide: no empty area reserved for copy. Warm ivory and pale lavender contemporary living room, rich cobalt accents. Across the left and middle two-thirds, show THREE LARGE realistic iPhones in a beautifully composed overlapping fan, upright at gentle different angles on a warm cream tabletop, nearly filling the banner height while all three phone screens stay substantially visible. These phones show distinct TEXT-FREE visual interpretations of the actual reference app: first a light pearl home screen with large moon album card, two big rounded artwork-only playlist tiles and a simple four-icon tab bar; second a light pearl library screen with a large family photo/album tile plus several large artwork-only playlist rows; third a deep indigo player screen with the moon-over-water record sleeve and exposed vinyl disc, a large light circular pause button flanked by previous and next symbols, waveform and heart icon. Use pure icons, realistic album art and shapes, zero letters/numbers. Preserve the app's cream-lavender and indigo design. At center foreground between phones and the person, a prominent palm-size cream circular MusicTag remote rests on a dark cobalt cushion: large blue central play/pause icon, separate cream previous and next buttons, two side volume symbol buttons, blue LED and small keyring hole; match reference 3's small circular remote silhouette. Across the right third, a naturally photographed elegant older East Asian woman around 70 with silver hair sits in the cobalt armchair in a bright welcoming home, wearing a warm ivory knit cardigan; her face and torso visible, a quiet authentic expression of enjoyment while one naturally proportioned hand presses the remote's center button. Show the connection between the tangible control and listening without arrows or labels. Soft sun through sheer curtains, warm wood, a few out-of-focus plants at far right. Photorealistic editorial product photography, tactile materials, convincing human anatomy and device proportions, sophisticated coherent lighting, crisp foreground screens and hardware with shallow depth behind. Richly fill the WHOLE 3:1 canvas with phones, hardware and human scene; no large blank region, no title typography, no poster borders, no graphic collage frames, no duplicate people, no floating detached hands. A confident immersive full-bleed product-and-use-context cover.
